Control Group of One

Early prototype of the experiment engine. It runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Design the experiment

3 cycles × (3 nights habit on + 3 nights habit off), order shuffled within each cycle so slow drifts (exam stress, a cold) hit both conditions.

2. Add your nights

Demo data is synthetic, with a known built-in effect. Or paste one line per night, in plan order: minutes asleep, followed (1 or 0)

3. Verdict

Privacy receipt

Voice: your browser's built-in voices by default. Paste the URL of the ElevenLabs proxy from cloud/ to hear ElevenLabs instead. It receives only a template name and a language, never health data.
